# Philosophy and provenance

Agora is not a claim that organizations and software are the same. It uses
organization/social theory as **design heuristics and critical checks** for a
plugin/skill/MCP management layer.

## Sources

- Mintzberg, *The Structuring of Organizations* (Prentice-Hall, 1979): contingency, differentiation/integration, coordination mechanisms.
- Laloux, *Reinventing Organizations* (Nelson Parker, 2014): self-management, wholeness, evolutionary purpose — used with governance guardrails, not as “remove all control”.
- Skelton & Pais, *Team Topologies* (IT Revolution, 2019): platform/enabling/stream-aligned/complicated-subsystem, cognitive load, team API.
- Bueno de Mesquita & Smith, *The Dictator's Handbook* (PublicAffairs, 2011): selectorate/winning-coalition as a warning about hidden control; not a literal model for software.
- Goffman, *The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life* (1959): front stage/back stage, impression management.
- Goffman, *Frame Analysis* (Harvard UP, 1974): frames, keying, fabrication, frame boundaries.
- Collins, *Interaction Ritual Chains* (Princeton UP, 2004): interaction rituals, emotional energy, symbols.
- Garfinkel, *Studies in Ethnomethodology* (Prentice-Hall, 1967): accountability, indexicality, breaching, practical reasoning.
- Durkheim, *The Division of Labour in Society* (1893): mechanical/organic solidarity, division of labour, anomie, integration.

## Evidence limit

The source research used legitimate public publisher/library metadata, official
previews, author materials and scholarly reviews. It did **not** include
pirated full texts, and not every book was read in full. Concepts are paraphrased;
formal citations must be checked against the original edition/page.

## Design commitments derived from the sources

1. Contingency over one best structure.
2. Differentiation plus integration.
3. Explicit roles and team APIs.
4. Frame before skill selection.
5. Accountable routing with a receipt.
6. Front-stage/back-stage separation.
7. Ritualized start/end without ritual replacing evidence.
8. Canonical ownership and anti-anomie rules.
9. User as principal; no hidden coalition controls defaults.
10. Fail-open: Agora unavailable must not break DSH.
